Current Initiatives

Zoshani Advisory is actively developing transformative initiatives across Africa that demonstrate our integrated approach to industrial development, technology transfer, and sustainable wealth creation. Details remain confidential during development phases.

West African Mineral Beneficiation Project

Structuring Phase

The Opportunity: Converting abundant raw mineral deposits into high-value manufactured products for regional and international markets, capturing exponentially more economic value in-country.

The Approach: Coordinating geological certification to international standards, structuring multi-source financing combining U.S. and European development capital, and facilitating technology transfer from American equipment manufacturers.

Impact Potential: Thousands of skilled manufacturing jobs, dramatically reduced dependence on imported industrial materials, permanent industrial capacity through comprehensive workforce training, and a replicable model for value-addition across African resource sectors.

Multi-Country Artisan Industrialization Program

Partnership Assembly

The Vision: Transforming informal artisan activity across four East African countries into a coordinated, export-oriented manufacturing ecosystem producing premium finished goods for global luxury markets.

The Model: Women-led enterprises equipped with American precision technology, trained through Centers of Excellence, connected to international buyers via unified market platforms, and certified for full ethical sourcing traceability commanding premium pricing.

Scale & Impact: Targeting thousands of women-led businesses, hundreds of millions in annual finished goods exports within five years, formal sector integration enabling access to financial services, and proof-of-concept applicable across multiple artisan manufacturing sectors continent-wide.

Bilateral Technology Transfer Acceleration Platform

Framework Development

The Challenge: African enterprises and governments face fragmented, slow, and inaccessible pathways to American and European industrial technology, while Western exporters struggle to navigate African markets profitably.

Our Solution: Comprehensive platform streamlining access to development finance while systematically matching African buyers with Western technology providers across agricultural processing, renewable energy systems, manufacturing automation, healthcare equipment, and digital infrastructure.

Strategic Objective: Facilitating billions in annual equipment exports creating American and European manufacturing jobs while building permanent African industrial capabilities—demonstrating that bilateral trade can be both commercially profitable and developmentally transformative.

Sector Transformation Capacity Programs

Ongoing

Beyond Individual Projects: Zoshani designs comprehensive capacity-building initiatives strengthening entire industrial sectors rather than isolated enterprises—creating multiplier effects that outlast any single intervention.

Current Focus: Agribusiness value chains (post-harvest processing and market linkages), renewable energy technical capacity (project development and maintenance), artisan manufacturing guild systems (quality assurance and collective marketing), and mining sector beneficiation (environmental management and community engagement).

Long-Term Impact: Permanent institutional capacity generating skilled operators, entrepreneurs, and technicians for decades—transforming how African countries build industrial competitiveness.
